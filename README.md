## 🛡️ What is TruthScan?

**TruthScan** is an AI-assisted content verification platform built around a single mission: **verify before you trust**. Instead of giving you a bare "true or false" verdict, TruthScan breaks a claim down, researches it, and returns a structured, evidence-based verification report you can actually reason about.

When you submit information to TruthScan, it **parses and analyzes the content**, then runs an **AI-assisted analysis** to understand what is actually being claimed. From there, it performs **online research** to find relevant, real-world sources and **collects evidence** that relates to the claim — not just a handful of links, but structured evidence that can be compared and weighed.

The heart of TruthScan is its ability to separate the signal from the noise. The platform **identifies supporting information** that backs the claim, **identifies contradicting information** that challenges it, and then **evaluates confidence** in the claim based on the weight of that evidence. It also performs a **risk assessment**, helping you understand the potential impact of acting on the information.

Finally, TruthScan **presents structured verification results** — clean, readable result boxes that summarize the claim, the evidence, the confidence level, the risk, and the sources, so you always know *why* a result looks the way it does.

> ⚠️ **Honest expectation setting:** TruthScan is an *AI-assisted tool*, not an oracle. It helps you investigate claims faster and more systematically — but the final judgment always belongs to you, supported by the evidence and sources it surfaces.

## 🧠 How It Works

TruthScan's architecture is a chain of focused stages — each one responsible for one part of turning a claim into a structured, evidence-backed result.

<div align="center">

```
  INPUT ──▶ PROCESS ──▶ AI ANALYZE ──▶ WEB RESEARCH ──▶ EVIDENCE
                                                          │
   RESULT ◀── RISK ◀── CONFIDENCE ◀── SUPPORTING/        │
                                     CONTRADICTING ◀─────┘
```

</div>

### 1️⃣ User submits content
A user pastes a piece of text (a headline, a social media post, a claim) or submits an image. TruthScan accepts the input and hands it to the pipeline.

### 2️⃣ Application processes the input
The input is cleaned and normalized — stripped of noise, formatted, and prepared so the analysis stage can work with a focused, well-defined claim rather than raw clutter.

### 3️⃣ AI analyzes the claim
The AI-assisted analysis engine examines the content and identifies the **core assertion** being made. This is a critical step: "analyze the claim" means understanding *what claim is actually being tested*, not just matching keywords.

### 4️⃣ Web research retrieves relevant information
With a clear claim in hand, TruthScan runs online research to find relevant, real-world information — reports, articles, publications, and other sources that speak to the subject.

### 5️⃣ Evidence is collected
Raw search results are refined into **evidence**: specific, relevant pieces of information tied to the claim, each connected to its source.

### 6️⃣ Supporting and contradicting information is separated
Evidence is sorted by direction. Findings that agree with the claim become **🟢 supporting data**; findings that challenge or qualify it become **🔴 contradicting data**. This both-sides structure is what makes TruthScan different from a simple verdict.

### 7️⃣ Confidence is evaluated
The relative weight of supporting vs. contradicting evidence is translated into a **confidence rating** — a clear signal of how strongly the available information backs the claim.

### 8️⃣ Risk is assessed
TruthScan evaluates the **risk** of trusting, sharing, or acting on the claim — factoring in evidence quality, claim strength, and the potential consequences of the information being wrong.

### 9️⃣ Final structured result is displayed
Everything is assembled into the **verification result panel**: the claim, the evidence, supporting and contradicting data, confidence, risk, and sources — organized so you can skim the verdict or dig into the details.

## 🧪 Example Verification

A worked example — this is what a real verification run looks like, stage by stage.

> **Claim:** *"Eating carrots dramatically improves night vision."*

```
USER CLAIM
   ▼
AI ANALYSIS          → Extracts the core assertion: carrots "dramatically"
                       improve night vision in healthy people.
   ▼
WEB RESEARCH         → Retrieves sources: NIH, WHO, ophthalmology publications,
                       nutrition science literature.
   ▼
EVIDENCE             → Vitamin A (from carrots, as beta-carotene) is essential
                       for retinal health. Severe vitamin A deficiency causes
                       night blindness.
   ▼
SUPPORTING DATA      → 🟢 Vitamin A supports healthy vision; correcting a real
                       deficiency restores night vision.
   ▼
CONTRADICTING DATA   → 🔴 No clinical evidence shows that extra carrots improve
                       night vision beyond normal in people who are not
                       deficient.
   ▼
CONFIDENCE           → 📊 LOW — the claim overstates the scientific consensus.
   ▼
RISK                 → ⚠️ LOW–MEDIUM — mostly harmless, but it spreads a
                       distorted version of the science.
   ▼
FINAL RESULT         → ⚠️ OVERSTATED / PARTIALLY SUPPORTED
```

The result panel then shows the full breakdown: the claim, the evidence, the green supporting data, the red contradicting data, the confidence rating, the risk, and the linked sources — everything you need to decide for yourself.

## 🎯 Why TruthScan?

Traditional verification stops at a verdict. TruthScan opens the black box.

      A single, bare verdict. No path, no evidence, no context. If it's wrong, you'll never know why.
    </td>
    <td style="background: rgba(0,229,255,0.05); border: 1px solid rgba(0,229,255,0.30); padding: 18px; text-align: center;">

TruthScan focuses on **context and evidence** rather than a binary stamp. That's the difference between being told what to think and being equipped to think.

## 🔒 Security & Privacy

TruthScan takes secrets and configuration seriously — the same discipline you'd expect from a security product.

| Practice | How TruthScan handles it |
|:---------|:-------------------------|
| 🔑 **Environment variables** | All sensitive configuration lives in environment variables, never in source code. |
| 🛡️ **API key protection** | Keys are stored in server-side configuration, outside the client bundle. |
| ⚙️ **Server-side functions** | AI analysis and web research run in server-side functions so secrets never reach the browser. |
| 🚫 **Never exposing secrets** | Public client keys are the only keys the frontend ever touches — and only the minimal anon key. |
| 🔐 **Secure configuration** | `.env` files are git-ignored; `.env.example` documents what's needed without revealing anything. |
| 🧾 **Commit hygiene** | Real credentials must never appear in commits — review diffs before pushing. |

> If you ever suspect a key was exposed, **rotate it immediately** and remove it from history.
